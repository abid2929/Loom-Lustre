About Loom & Lustre

Loom & Lustre brings you hand-picked sarees, kurtis, westernwear, shoes, bags, jewellery and beauty that are original, well made and fairly priced. Every order is checked by hand before it leaves our hands.

Delivery & payment

Inside Dhaka: ৳70 (1 to 2 days). Outside Dhaka: ৳130 (2 to 4 days). Orders over ৳3,000 get free delivery.

You pay cash to the delivery person when you receive your parcel. We call every customer to confirm the order before we send it.

Exchange policy

Please check your parcel in front of the delivery person. If a product is damaged, leaking or not what you ordered, tell us the same day and we will replace it. For hygiene reasons, opened or used beauty products cannot be returned.

Original products

We only sell sealed, original products. Each order is checked by hand and packed with care.
